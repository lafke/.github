# Support

- **Documentation**: [lafke.dev](https://lafke.dev) and the `docs/` folder of
  [lafke/lafke](https://github.com/lafke/lafke).
- **Bugs and feature requests**: open an issue on the repository the problem belongs to,
  usually [lafke/lafke](https://github.com/lafke/lafke/issues). Include the output of
  `lafke doctor` and the `lafke` version.
- **Security issues**: do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else**: write to hello@lafke.dev.
